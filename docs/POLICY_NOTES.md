# Policy contradictions to resolve

These conflicts exist in the source policy pages (shipping, shipping-policy, returns-and-replacements, vip-warranty) and related pages (faq, why-shop-with-us, about-us-jestik, become-a-dealer). The rewritten policy pages keep the original terms as written. None of these conflicts were resolved.

## Shipping

1. **Free shipping scope.** `shipping` says *all* products ship free (freight truck or ground) within the 48 contiguous states. The FAQ, why-shop-with-us, and about-us pages say the same. `shipping-policy` says free shipping does **not** apply to orders with corporate discount pricing, **tables, or chairs**, and that the order must be placed through the website. It also says "within the US", not "48 contiguous states".
2. **Delivery and processing times.**
   - `shipping` and `faq`: processing and packaging takes 1–7 business days, and you should "allow 1–7 business days within the US". `shipping` also says many products are ready to ship within two days.
   - `shipping-policy`: most orders arrive within about 5–7 business days of the order. Orders ship within 48 hours (24 hours if everything is in stock). Special order and non-stock items, **ergonomic chairs, and adjustable tables** have a 2–5 week lead time.
   - These do not agree, and the 2–5 week lead time for chairs and tables appears only in `shipping-policy`.
3. **Ship-from location.** Only `shipping-policy` mentions the Rancho Dominguez, CA warehouse. Other pages give no origin.
4. **Military addresses.** `shipping` and `faq` say no "APO/FPO" addresses. `shipping-policy` says "government ARO addresses", which is probably a typo for APO. It was kept as written.
5. **Alaska/Hawaii and other non-contiguous areas.** `shipping` says additional charges apply to Guam, Alaska, Puerto Rico, Hawaii, the Virgin Islands, and international addresses, and to contact you for an estimate. `shipping-policy` says to request a quote for Alaska and Hawaii only. These do not truly conflict, but the wording and coverage differ.
6. **Refused shipments.** `shipping` reserves the right to bill for refused shipments, and `returns-and-replacements` (Cancellations) says not to refuse packages. The same returns page also says customers may refuse heavily damaged freight deliveries. Consider clarifying that the billing rule excludes refusals of damaged freight.
7. **Insurance.** why-shop-with-us says all outbound shipments are insured. No policy page states this.

## Returns, refunds and exchanges

8. **Return authorization name.** `returns-and-replacements` requires an **ARN** (Authorization Return Number) obtained by replying to an emailed request. It also calls this a "Return Authorization Number". `shipping-policy` requires an **RMA number** obtained from sales@jestik.com.
9. **Bulk-order return threshold.** `returns-and-replacements` states two different rules:
   - Non-Refundable Items: bulk orders of **5 or more pieces**, or single-receipt orders of **at least $2,500.00**, are non-refundable.
   - Items Not Eligible for Return: bulk orders of **10 pieces or more of the same product**, or orders **greater than $2,500**.
   - The quantity (5 vs. 10), the "same product" qualifier, and the $2,500 boundary ("at least" vs. "greater than") all differ. Volume discounts also start at 5 or more products.
10. **Full refund vs. deductions.** The Refunds section says "we will fully refund your order". Elsewhere the page says a restocking fee **will** be deducted from every return ("Returns with Restocking Fees"), a restocking fee **may** apply (Damaged & Defective), and non-refundable fees apply. `shipping-policy` says return shipping cost will be deducted from the refund. The FAQ says "refund less applicable fees and charges". why-shop-with-us says a restocking fee "may" be charged on non-defective returns and promises a refund "within 30 days, no questions asked".
11. **Return condition.** The 30-day guarantee allows returns in "nearly-new" condition. Elsewhere the page requires "good, like-new condition", and the non-refundable list excludes anything "used". Exchanges require "like-new".
12. **When the 30 days start.** The page uses "receipt of your product" in one place and "after it has been delivered" (FAQ) in another. These are probably the same, but the wording differs.
13. **Return form.** Returns require the Return Form "found in your package", and a missing return shipping form makes an item non-refundable. `shipping-policy` mentions no form.

## Warranties

14. **Lifetime warranty vs. 1-year warranty.** `returns-and-replacements`, why-shop-with-us, and become-a-dealer promise a **full lifetime warranty** (return, repair, or replace "at any time after purchase"). The FAQ describes a **1 Year Manufacturer's Replacement Warranty** against factory defects. The relationship between the lifetime warranty, the 1-year warranty, and the VIP Warranty is never explained.
15. **Lifetime "return at any time" vs. 30-day returns.** The lifetime warranty says a product can be "returned ... at any time after purchase", which conflicts with the 30-day return window and the non-returnable categories.
16. **VIP Warranty "no matter the cause" vs. exclusions.** `vip-warranty` promises repair or replacement at no charge regardless of "how, why, whose fault" and "no matter the cause". The same page excludes loss, theft, deliberate damage, and cosmetic damage.
17. **VIP Warranty vs. return-shipping rules.** The VIP Warranty promises "absolutely no charges" for repair or replacement. The returns and shipping-policy pages put return shipping costs on the customer. They do not say whether warranty returns are an exception.

## Damage reporting deadlines

18. **Small parcel damage window.** The same section says "within 7 working days", then "within 7 days", then "seven (7) business days of receipt".
19. **Freight damage.** You must notify immediately, and carriers allow claims within 48 hours of delivery. This is consistent internally, but nothing says how freight damage relates to the 7-day small-parcel rule.
