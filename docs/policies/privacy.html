This Privacy Policy explains how Jestik, Inc. ("Jestik", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you visit or make a purchase from www.jestik.com (the "Site").

Information we collect

Device information. When you visit the Site, we automatically collect information about your device, including your web browser, IP address, time zone, and some of the cookies installed on your device. As you browse, we also collect the pages and products you view, the websites or search terms that referred you, and how you interact with the Site. We collect this using:

  • Cookies: small data files placed on your device, often with an anonymous unique identifier. To learn how to disable cookies, visit allaboutcookies.org.
  • Log files: records of actions on the Site, including IP address, browser type, internet service provider, referring and exit pages, and date and time stamps.
  • Web beacons, tags, and pixels: small files used to record how you browse the Site, including those provided by our analytics and advertising partners.

Order information. When you make or attempt a purchase, we collect your name, company name (if provided), billing and shipping addresses, payment information (credit card details and other payment methods offered at checkout), email address, and phone number.

Information you send us. When you request a quote, contact us, apply for a dealer or volume account, or submit a tax-exemption certificate, we collect the information you provide.

How we use your information

  • To process payments, ship orders, and send invoices and order confirmations
  • To respond to questions, quotes, returns, and warranty requests
  • To screen orders for potential risk or fraud
  • To improve the Site, for example by analyzing how customers browse and measuring our marketing
  • Where you have agreed, to send you product information, offers, or advertising

Sharing your information

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run our business, such as Shopify (our e-commerce platform, see shopify.com/legal/privacy), payment processors, shipping carriers, manufacturers that fulfill or warranty your order, and analytics providers such as Google Analytics (opt out at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). We may also share information to comply with the law, respond to lawful requests, or protect our rights. We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

We may use your information to show you relevant ads. You can opt out of targeted advertising at facebook.com/settings/?tab=ads, google.com/settings/ads, and the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out portal at optout.aboutads.info.

Do Not Track

We do not change our data collection practices when we see a Do Not Track signal from your browser.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including California and the European Economic Area, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of certain uses. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Data retention

We keep order information for our records, including tax and warranty purposes, unless and until you ask us to delete it, subject to legal requirements.

Minors

The Site is not intended for individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or for legal or regulatory reasons.
