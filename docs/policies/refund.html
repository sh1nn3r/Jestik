We want you to love your new workspace. If something isn't right, here is how returns and refunds work. Full details are on our Returns and Replacements page.

30-day returns

You may return most products within 30 days of delivery. Items must be complete, in like-new condition, and in their original packaging, and must not have been installed, used, or modified where applicable.

How to start a return

  1. Email sales@jestik.com or call (800) 592-8250 (Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM PST) with your order number and the item you want to return.
  2. We will reply with an Authorization Return Number (ARN) and return instructions.
  3. Write the ARN on the return form and package, and ship the item using a trackable service.

We cannot process returns without an ARN. Please do not send items back to the manufacturer.

Non-returnable items

  • Items made to order, customized, assembled, or modified
  • Items excluded from return by their manufacturer
  • Items returned without original packaging, with missing parts, or without an ARN

Bulk orders

Orders of 5 or more pieces, or a single order totaling $2,500 or more, may be subject to separate return terms. Contact us before returning a bulk order.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will email you to confirm whether it is approved. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Return shipping costs and non-refundable charges such as international shipping and duties are not refunded.

Damaged, defective, or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we will arrange a replacement at no cost to you. Every product is also covered by our 1-year warranty; see the VIP Warranty page.
